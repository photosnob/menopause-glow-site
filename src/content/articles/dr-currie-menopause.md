## Beyond Hot Flashes: Dr. Currie Menopause Care for the Whole Woman

While temperature regulation gone haywire might be the poster child of menopause symptoms, Dr. Currie's practice addresses the full spectrum of changes women experience.

### The Brain Fog Phenomenon

"I used to be sharp as a tack, but now I can't remember why I walked into a room!" This common complaint receives serious attention in Dr. Currie's menopause treatment protocols.

Cognitive changes during menopause aren't imaginary—they're backed by science. Research from the Women's Brain Health Initiative shows that estrogen decline affects neurotransmitter function and even brain structure. Dr. Currie menopause care includes cognitive support strategies:

- Targeted supplements like omega-3 fatty acids and phosphatidylserine
- Sleep optimization techniques (because sleep deprivation compounds cognitive issues)
- Mental exercise recommendations to maintain neural pathways

### The Mood Rollercoaster

"It's not just about feeling sad or irritable," Dr. Currie notes. "Menopause-related mood changes can include anxiety, panic attacks, and even feelings of detachment that women have never experienced before."

The Dr. Currie menopause approach to emotional wellbeing might include:

- Bioidentical hormone support when appropriate
- Adaptogenic herbs that help regulate stress response
- Mindfulness practices specifically designed for menopause-related mood fluctuations
- Referrals to therapists specializing in midlife women's issues when needed

The dreaded menopause middle is a common concern in Dr. Currie's office. While hormonal shifts do influence fat distribution and metabolism, Dr. Currie menopause weight management strategies have helped countless women maintain healthy body composition.

## The Dr. Currie Menopause Timeline: What to Expect When You're Not Expecting (Periods)

One of the most valuable aspects of Dr. Currie's approach is providing a roadmap through the menopause transition. Rather than viewing it as one event, Dr. Currie educates patients about the distinct phases:

### Perimenopause: The Rollercoaster Years

"This is when women often feel most confused," Dr. Currie notes. "They're having symptoms but still having periods, sometimes irregularly. Many doctors miss this diagnosis entirely."

The Dr. Currie menopause care model identifies perimenopause early, often years before the final period, allowing for proactive management of symptoms when they first appear.

### Menopause: The Turning Point

By definition, menopause is marked by 12 consecutive months without a period. Dr. Currie helps women understand that while this is a significant milestone, it's just one point in a longer journey.

### Postmenopause: The New Normal

"This is where we focus on long-term health optimization," explains Dr. Currie. "Menopause care doesn't end when hot flashes subside. We're looking at bone health, cardiovascular protection, and cognitive preservation for decades to come."

Bonafide Relizen is a hormone-free supplement designed to alleviate hot flashes, one of the most common and uncomfortable symptoms of menopause. Derived from a plant-based source, Relizen provides a natural alternative to hormone replacement therapy (HRT) for women seeking relief without the risks associated with synthetic hormones. But how does it work, and is it effective? Let’s explore the key aspects of this product and its potential benefits.

### **What is Relizen?**

Relizen’s active ingredient is derived from the pollen of the Swedish flower *Secale cereale* (rye), which has been used in Europe for decades to manage menopause symptoms. The supplement is formulated to target hot flashes specifically but is also promoted as a remedy for night sweats, irritability, and disrupted sleep patterns. Bonafide, the company behind Relizen, markets it as a safe, natural solution that balances menopausal symptoms without the use of estrogen or other hormones.

### **How Does Relizen Work?**

The plant-based pollen extract in Relizen is thought to regulate the body’s temperature control system, thus reducing the frequency and intensity of hot flashes. While the exact mechanism of action is still being researched, the supplement is believed to work on the hypothalamus, the part of the brain responsible for regulating body temperature.

Unlike many other menopause supplements that rely on phytoestrogens (plant compounds that mimic estrogen), Relizen is hormone-free. This makes it a suitable option for women who cannot or do not wish to take hormone-based treatments due to medical conditions or personal preference.

### **Effectiveness and User Reviews**

Relizen has garnered positive reviews from users, many of whom report a significant reduction in the severity and frequency of their hot flashes after taking the supplement for several weeks. According to a survey by Bonafide, 84% of women who tried Relizen said it improved their quality of life by reducing their menopause symptoms​. Women have noted improvements not only in hot flashes but also in sleep quality, mood stability, and energy levels.

However, as with any supplement, individual experiences vary. While some women report quick and noticeable improvements, others may take longer to experience relief or may not find it as effective.

### **Scientific Findings and Safety**

The pollen extract used in Relizen has been studied in clinical trials, particularly in Europe. A study published in *Climacteric*, the official journal of the International Menopause Society, found that the active ingredient in Relizen significantly reduced hot flashes and improved sleep quality without causing any hormonal side effects​.

Additionally, since Relizen is hormone-free, it doesn’t carry the same risks as HRT, such as an increased likelihood of breast cancer or cardiovascular events. It is considered safe for long-term use, with few reported side effects.
